# United States Remote Work & Travel Policy 2024 (EXPIRED)
Document Version: 2024.1
Effective Date: 2024-01-01
Expiration Date: 2024-12-31
Region: US
Category: Remote Work

## 1.0 Policy Expiration Warning
NOTICE: This policy expired on December 31, 2024. It is retained only for historical compliance and audit purposes and should NOT be used for active employee decisions.

## 2.0 Historical Remote Work Stipend
2.1 During the 2024 fiscal year, US employees received a monthly internet subsidy of $50.
2.2 Remote workers were permitted to work outside their home state for up to 30 days per calendar year.
