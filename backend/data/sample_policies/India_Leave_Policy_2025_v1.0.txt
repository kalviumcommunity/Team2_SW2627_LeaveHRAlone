# India Leave Policy 2025 (v1.0 - Superseded)
Document Version: 2025.1
Effective Date: 2025-01-01
Expiration Date: 2025-12-31
Region: India
Category: Leave

## 1.0 Overview
This was the 2025 leave policy applicable to India employees. This version is obsolete and replaced by Version 2026.1.

## 2.0 Annual Leave
2.1 Full-time employees were entitled to 18 annual paid leaves per calendar year.
2.2 A maximum of 5 unused annual paid leaves could be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year.

## 3.0 Sick Leave
3.1 Employees received 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
