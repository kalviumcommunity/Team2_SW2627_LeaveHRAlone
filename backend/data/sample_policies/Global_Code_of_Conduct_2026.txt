# Global Code of Conduct & Workplace Ethics 2026
Document Version: 2026.1
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Region: Global
Category: Workplace Policies

## 1.0 Global Applicability
This Code of Conduct applies globally to all employees, contractors, executives, and directors across all operational regions (India, United States, United Kingdom, Singapore, and remote locations worldwide).

## 2.0 Equal Opportunity & Anti-Harassment
2.1 PolicyPilot AI operates an absolute zero-tolerance policy towards any form of harassment, discrimination, bullying, or retaliation based on race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or age.
2.2 All employees have the right to a respectful and psychologically safe workplace environment.
2.3 Any instance of discriminatory conduct will result in immediate disciplinary review up to and including termination of employment.

## 3.0 Confidentiality & Data Protection
3.1 Employees must strictly safeguard company proprietary information, intellectual property, customer records, and employee personal data in compliance with GDPR, HIPAA, and relevant data privacy legislation.
3.2 Customer data and internal policies must never be input into unauthorized third-party generative AI tools without explicit prior approval from the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO).

## 4.0 Whistleblower Protection
4.1 The company provides a confidential, anonymous reporting hotline (whistleblower@policypilot.internal) for reporting ethical violations, fraud, or policy breaches.
4.2 Retaliation of any kind against an employee who reports a concern in good faith is strictly prohibited by law and company policy.
