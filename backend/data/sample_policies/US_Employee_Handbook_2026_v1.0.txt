# United States Employee Handbook 2026
Document Version: 2026.1
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Region: US
Category: Workplace Policies

## 1.0 Introduction & Employment At-Will
This handbook sets forth the policies, benefits, and workplace standards for employees residing and working in the United States. Employment is at-will.

## 2.0 Paid Time Off (PTO) & Federal Holidays
2.1 Full-time US employees receive 15 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per year, accrued at 1.25 days per month of service.
2.2 The company observes 10 official US Federal Holidays: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Day after Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day.
2.3 US employees can carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the following calendar year. Any additional accrued PTO must be utilized by December 31st.

## 3.0 Healthcare & Dental Benefits
3.1 The company offers comprehensive medical, dental, and vision coverage through Blue Cross Blue Shield.
3.2 Employees may choose between a PPO plan and a High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) with Health Savings Account (HSA) contribution matching up to $1,000 annually.
3.3 Dependent coverage is available for legal spouses and children up to age 26.

## 4.0 401(k) Retirement Savings Plan
4.1 Eligible US employees may participate in the 401(k) retirement savings plan starting on their first day of employment.
4.2 The company provides a 100% dollar-for-dollar match on employee contributions up to 4% of eligible base salary. Company matching funds vest immediately at 100%.

## 5.0 Family and Medical Leave (FMLA)
5.1 In accordance with federal law, eligible employees with at least 12 months of service who have worked 1,250 hours are eligible for up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected FMLA leave per 12-month period.
