# India Benefits & Health Insurance Policy 2026
Document Version: 2026.1
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Region: India
Category: Benefits & Insurance

## 1.0 Group Medical Insurance (GMC)
1.1 All regular full-time employees are covered under the company Group Medical Insurance plan from day one of employment.
1.2 Coverage details:
- Sum insured: ₹5,00,000 (Five Lakhs INR) family floater per policy year.
- Covered dependents: Employee, legal spouse, up to 2 dependent children (up to age 25), and dependent parents or parent-in-laws.
- Pre-existing conditions are covered from day one without waiting period.
- Maternity coverage under GMC is capped at ₹75,000 for normal delivery and ₹1,00,000 for caesarean section.

## 2.0 Outpatient Department (OPD) & Dental Benefit
2.1 Employees can claim reimbursement for OPD consultations, diagnostic tests, and prescribed dental care up to ₹15,000 per financial year upon submitting verified tax invoices.

## 3.0 Annual Wellness Stipend
3.1 Each employee in India is allocated an annual wellness allowance of ₹20,000, claimable for gym memberships, fitness trackers, mental wellness sessions, or ergonomically certified home office furniture.

## 4.0 Life and Personal Accident Insurance
4.1 Group Term Life (GTL) insurance provides coverage equivalent to 3x of annual CTC.
4.2 Group Personal Accident (GPA) coverage provides 2x of annual CTC in the event of accidental permanent disability or loss of life.
